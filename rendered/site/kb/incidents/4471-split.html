Why it looked like one bug

  • Merged by subject. Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. “Wrong date” reads the same whatever caused it, so three problems landed in one ticket.

  • The desk saw the one-day moves after #4471 was marked known and noted them, but only as a footnote.
