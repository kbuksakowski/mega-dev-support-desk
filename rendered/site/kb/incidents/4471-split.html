How each report is placed:

  1. Duplicates out first. The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. One id, one report.
  2. By tenant, not by intake tag. The tenant’s locale and booking form decide the cause (causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first. Reports through a booking-v3 form are the form hint; everything else is the shared parser.
  3. One-day moves. Reports the desk itself noted as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) are their own problem: day and month did not swap, so neither the parser nor the form hint explains them. The desk counts a report where it is filed and footnotes the reading, so they stay in the parser count too.

Why it looked like one bug

  • Merged by subject. Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. “Wrong date” reads the same whatever caused it, so three problems landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags are a guess. Intake tags a report hint when the reporter’s browser is en-US and parser otherwise. The browser is not the tenant: app and email reports from en-US browsers get hint, and form reports from a booking-v3 tenant’s own locale get parser. Counting by tag gives the wrong size for both.
  • The desk saw the one-day moves and noted them, but only as a footnote under the parser, with no ticket of their own.
